Hotel Features

World-class Amenities

The Akwanga Pavilion: Signature Dining

Our award-winning hotel restaurant offers an unparalleled fine-dining experience. Overlooking the lush Nasarawa valleys, The Akwanga Pavilion serves contemporary Nigerian cuisine interwoven with classic Michelin-level techniques, utilizing fresh local produce and premium proteins, perfectly paired by our master Sommelier.

Midnight Infinity Oasis

A sweeping, temperature-controlled infinity pool that merges seamlessly with the horizon. Outfitted with sunken fire pits and private cabanas, the Oasis remains open late into the evening for uninterrupted stargazing over the Nasarawa plains.

Subterranean Tasting Vault

Descend into our limestone-carved wine cellar. Curated by our Executive Sommelier, the vault houses an extensive collection of rare vintages and offers private, candle-lit tasting sessions paired with artisanal local cheeses and cured meats.

Tranquil Spa & Wellness

Suspended above a natural lake on the estate, our wellness sanctuary offers holistic therapies and deep-tissue treatments designed to rejuvenate your body. Experience our signature solid-mineral clay wraps and listen to the gentle breeze of the savannah.

High-Performance Athletics & Turf

Maintain your stamina with our state-of-the-art fitness center. Complete with premium resistance equipment, a comprehensive range of cardio machines, and an outdoor athletic sprint turf perfect for agility drills and conditioning in the fresh air.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to the hotel from Abuja airport?
L'Horizon is accessible via a smooth luxury chauffeur drive directly from Nnamdi Azikiwe International Airport (Abuja) to our estate in Nasarawa. Our VIP transfer service can be added to your booking at checkout.
Are children allowed at the hotel?
Yes, children are welcome. We recommend our Family & Estates collection for guests traveling with younger children, as they offer secure, enclosed garden pools and expansive play areas within the estate.
